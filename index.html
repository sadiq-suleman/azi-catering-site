Hello, we're Azi Catering

Serious about food. Relaxed about everything else.

It started as cooking for friends' weddings and birthdays, and honestly it still feels like that. A small, seasonal catering studio that treats a dinner for twelve with the same care as a wedding for a hundred. Menus shift with the seasons, ingredients come from growers we actually know, and planning feels less like a transaction and more like sitting down with someone who's got you.
