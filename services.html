Weddings

A wedding menu that tastes like you

Your day, your flavors. We start with a tasting, design a menu around the two of you, and run the whole thing on the day so you never touch a serving spoon. From plated fine-dining to a relaxed family-style feast.

Starting from $85 per person.

  • Complimentary menu consultation and private tasting
  • Custom menu design, printed menu cards
  • Cocktail hour canapés or grazing station
  • A trusted server or two to help serve on the day
  • Plates, flatware, glassware, and linen coordination
  • Cake cutting, coffee service, and full cleanup

How it works

Four steps from hello to dessert

01 · Tell us

Share your date, headcount, and vibe through our quick inquiry form. We reply within a business day.

02 · Design & taste

We propose a menu and quote, then refine it together — with a tasting for weddings and larger events.

03 · Confirm

Lock the date with a deposit. I take care of prep, rentals, and logistics from here.

04 · Enjoy

We arrive, set up, serve, and clean up. You get to be a guest at your own event.
