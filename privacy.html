Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Azi Catering is a small, independent catering studio. We only collect what we need to plan your event and reply to you — nothing more. This page explains it in plain English.

Who we are

Azi Catering ("we", "us") is a seasonal catering studio based in Brampton, Ontario, serving the Greater Toronto Area. If you have any questions about this policy or your information, email azicateringgta@gmail.com.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us — mainly through the inquiry form or by emailing us. That can include:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your preferred way to be contacted
  • Event details you share — date, event type, guest count, cuisine preferences, and dietary needs
  • Anything else you write in the notes or in an email to us

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we don't ask for sensitive information beyond the dietary and allergy details you volunteer so we can cook safely for you.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a quote or proposal
  • To plan, cook, and deliver your event safely (including allergies and dietary needs)
  • To keep in touch about your booking

We do not sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How your inquiry reaches us

When you submit the inquiry form, the details are sent to us by email (or through a form-handling service that passes them to our inbox). That provider processes the data on our behalf solely to deliver your message. We recommend not sending anything you'd consider highly confidential through a web form.

Who we share it with

We keep things small and rarely share anything. When we do, it's limited to what's needed to run your event — for example, a trusted person helping serve on the day, or a rental supplier — and only the details relevant to them. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry and booking details for as long as needed to plan your event and for a reasonable period afterward for our records, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner (see below).

Your choices & rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, or delete it — just email azicateringgta@gmail.com and we'll take care of it. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under laws such as Canada's PIPEDA (or the GDPR); we're happy to honor them.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. For the small technical details, see our Cookies Notice.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

This notice is provided in good faith and in plain language. It isn't legal advice — if you have specific legal obligations, please have a professional review it before launch.
