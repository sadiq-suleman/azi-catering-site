Cookies Notice

Last updated: 8 July 2026

Short version: this website does not use tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. It stores one small thing on your device — only to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser. Similar technologies (like "local storage") work the same way. They're often used to remember settings or to track people across sites — we don't do the tracking kind.

What this site stores

NameTypePurposeExpires
tlt-cookie-consent Local storage (not a cookie) Remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice, so it doesn't reappear on every visit. Until you clear your browser storage

That's the entire list. No cookies are set for advertising, and we don't build a profile of you.

Third-party services

We load fonts from Google Fonts and some photography from an image host, which means your browser contacts those services to fetch files. They may see your IP address as part of a normal web request, but this site does not place tracking cookies through them.

If we add analytics in the future (for example, to see which pages are popular), we'll list it here first and ask for your consent before any tracking cookies are set.

Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage anytime in your browser settings. Because this site doesn't rely on them to function, blocking them won't break anything — the notice may just reappear.

More information

For how we handle the details you send us, see our Privacy Policy.
